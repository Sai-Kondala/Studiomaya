# Design System
Studio Maya - Clean. Minimal. Premium.

## 1. Design Principles
- **Minimal & Clean**: Reduce visual clutter. Focus heavily on product imagery and crisp typography.
- **Trust & Credibility**: Professional UI elements, smooth loading states, and clear error handling during checkout.
- **Responsive**: Perfect rendering across mobile, tablet, and desktop without horizontal scrolling or clipping.

## 2. Color Palette
- **Primary**: Black (`#000000`) for primary buttons and strong emphasis.
- **Backgrounds**: White (`#ffffff`) and very light grays (`#f9fafb`) for subtle separation (e.g., Cart Drawer).
- **Text**: Dark gray (`text-gray-900`) for headings, lighter gray (`text-gray-500`) for secondary information.
- **Accents**: Red (`#fee2e2` / `#b91c1c`) for error states and validation.

## 3. Typography
- Modern sans-serif stack (default Tailwind sans).
- **Headings**: Bold, high contrast.
- **Buttons**: Medium font weight, tracking-normal, with `whitespace-nowrap` to prevent awkward wrapping on small screens.

## 4. Components
- **Buttons**: Fully rounded or softly rounded corners (`rounded-xl`). Hover states should slightly darken (`hover:bg-gray-800`).
- **Cards**: Subtle borders (`border-gray-100`) and soft shadows (`shadow-sm`) to lift items off the page.
