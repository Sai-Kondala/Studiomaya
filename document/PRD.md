# Product Requirements Document (PRD)
Studio Maya - Digital Product Store

## 1. Product Overview
Studio Maya is a modern digital product store that allows users to discover and purchase premium digital assets (like Notion templates, PDF guides, etc.). The platform provides a seamless checkout experience and automatic email delivery of purchased files.

## 2. Problem Statement
Creators need a simple, fast, and secure way to sell digital products without relying on heavy third-party platforms that take large cuts. Customers expect a frictionless checkout experience with instant access to their purchases.

## 3. Goals
- Provide a beautiful, minimalist storefront to display digital products.
- Enable a frictionless, secure checkout flow using Razorpay.
- Support a shopping cart for multiple product purchases.
- Automate file delivery via secure email links.
- Provide a secure admin dashboard to manage products, view orders, and track customers.

## 4. Target Users
- **Customers**: People looking for premium digital tools, templates, and guides.
- **Admin/Creator**: The store owner who needs to add products, track sales, and manage store settings.

## 5. Core Features
1. **Product Storefront**: Clean grid layout of available digital products.
2. **Shopping Cart**: Client-side persistent cart with drawer UI.
3. **Checkout**: Secure payment integration with Razorpay.
4. **Automated Delivery**: Immediate email delivery of digital assets upon successful payment via Resend.
5. **Admin Dashboard**: Secure area to manage products, view orders, and see a unique customer list.
